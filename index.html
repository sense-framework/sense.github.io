Research program

One framework, several interacting scales.

The program studies mechanisms that are usually separated: cognition, physiological regulation, local tissue dynamics, memory, development, embodiment, and durable continuity.

01

Continuous state

The system advances as one history-dependent process rather than resetting its effective identity at every interaction.

02

Embodied causality

Learning is evaluated through consequences in an environment, including energy, risk, damage, recovery, and goal progress.

03

Local learning

Research focuses on locally available activity, eligibility, modulation, replay, and delayed consequence rather than relying only on global credit assignment.

04

Developmental change

Fast adaptation and slower structural consolidation are treated as distinct timescales with validation, provenance, and rollback.

Research discipline

Claims follow evidence.

SENSE is experimental research. The work does not, by itself, establish general intelligence, subjective consciousness, biological equivalence, or superiority to established machine-learning architectures. Failed mechanisms and negative results are part of the record rather than being hidden.
